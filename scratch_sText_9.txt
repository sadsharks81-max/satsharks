Q1 | Correct Answer: B Skill: Central Idea | Difficulty: Medium
Why B is correct: The passage states that Utamaro “earned wide recognition”
for his portraiture work in bijin-ga, that his “commitment to this subject was so
influential” that later artists worked “in the manner of Utamaro.” This directly

Trap analysis: - A) Wrong detail - the passage says Utamaro focused on

before him. - D) Unsupported - the passage says later artists imitated Utamaro,
not that they surpassed him.
