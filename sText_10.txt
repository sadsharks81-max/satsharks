word might mean in isolation. The narrator contrasts what he was told to do with what he actually did.

Question 16

Question 17
Correct Answer: C) stems
The subject is "this capability" (singular), and we need a finite verb. "Stems" is a present-tense, singular verb that
agrees with the singular subject and creates a complete clause: "this capability stems from..."
Why other choices fail: "Stemming" (A) and "having stemmed" (B) are participles that cannot serve as the main verb. "To
stem" (D) is an infinitive that cannot serve as the main verb of the clause.
Tip: Identify the subject and check whether you need a finite verb or a modifier. "This capability" is the subject and needs a
conjugated verb. "Stemming" and "having stemmed" are participles; "to stem" is an infinitive. Only "stems" is a finite verb.
Question 18
Correct Answer: B) languages—
The sentence contains a closing dash before "and accounts for." To create a properly paired parenthetical, you need
an opening dash after "400 languages." This creates: "comprises more than 400 languages—Mandarin and

Tip: When you see a closing dash later in a sentence, look for where the parenthetical begins and place a matching
opening dash there. Paired dashes work like paired parentheses.
